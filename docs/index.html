Keyboard layout

Press the letter of a face to turn it, and hold Shift to turn it the other way. Each picture shows the layer that moves (orange) and which way it goes. Letters follow your screen: U is always the face on top. Tap a picture to make that turn.

Rest both hands on the home row like typing (index fingers on F and J). Every key is one turn, no Shift needed. H is a turn in this layout, so use the Hint button for hints.
