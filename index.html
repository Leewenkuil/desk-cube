Keyboard layout

Press the letter of a face to turn it, and hold Shift to turn it the other way.

Rest both hands on the home row like typing (index fingers on F and J). Every key is one turn, no Shift needed. H is a turn here, so use the Hint button for hints.

Slow Instant

Sounds

A click on each turn, a chime when the cube is solved, and beeps at 8 and 12 seconds of inspection.

Sticker colours

U is always the top of your screen · orange = the layer that turns · ⇧ = Shift
